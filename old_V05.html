    The game

    Everything you can do

    36power fruits 7fighting styles 18skin bundles 12faces

    Power fruits

    Hold a fruit in your hand, eat it and get its powers. Every fruit has its own moves and a stronger V2.

    Workshop

    Mine materials and forge your own swords and pickaxes.

    Cars

    Buy a car, park it anywhere and drive across the town.

    Bank robbery

    Rob the bank with your crew. The police are coming in one minute.

    Races

    Race on the tracks, beat your best time and win coins.

    The sea

    Go down to the sea, find islands and fight sea bosses.

    Caves and treasure

    Dig deep, open treasure chests and find secret places.

    Play in Arabic

    The whole game works in English and Arabic. Switch from the phone menu.
